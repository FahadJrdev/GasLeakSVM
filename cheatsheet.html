In plain words

Seven cheap sensors → seven numbers per reading → scale them → an RBF-kernel SVM with \(C=100\), \(\gamma=10\) → one of four classes, in about a millisecond, 98.23% correct on unseen readings. Main limit: sensor drift (54.37% on later readings). Pipeline case study: leak sound → 39 Fourier features → the same kind of SVM → pipe pressure 88.35% correct on real gas-pipe leak recordings.

Formulas to write

$$\text{margin}=\frac{2}{\|\mathbf{w}\|}$$
$$\min_{\mathbf{w},b,\boldsymbol\xi}\ \tfrac12\|\mathbf{w}\|^2+C\sum_i\xi_i\quad\text{s.t.}\quad y_i(\mathbf{w}^\top\phi(\mathbf{x}_i)+b)\ge1-\xi_i,\ \xi_i\ge0$$
$$\mathbf{w}=\sum_i\alpha_iy_i\phi(\mathbf{x}_i),\qquad\sum_i\alpha_iy_i=0,\qquad0\le\alpha_i\le C$$
$$\max_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\ \sum_i\alpha_i-\tfrac12\sum_{i,j}\alpha_i\alpha_jy_iy_jK(\mathbf{x}_i,\mathbf{x}_j)$$
$$\alpha_i\big[y_if(\mathbf{x}_i)-1+\xi_i\big]=0,\qquad (C-\alpha_i)\xi_i=0$$
$$K(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{z})=e^{-\gamma\|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{z}\|^2},\qquad f(\mathbf{x})=\sum_{i\in SV}\alpha_iy_iK(\mathbf{x}_i,\mathbf{x})+b$$
$$z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma},\qquad N_{\text{OvO}}=\frac{k(k-1)}{2}=6,\qquad T_{\text{predict}}=O(n_{SV}d)$$
$$P=\frac{TP}{TP+FP},\quad R=\frac{TP}{TP+FN},\quad F1=\frac{2PR}{P+R}$$
$$S_k=\sum_{n}(s_n-\bar s)e^{-2\pi i kn/N},\qquad E_b=\log\Big(1+\sum_{k\in\mathcal B_b}|S_k|^2\Big)$$

Sentences to say

What is an SVM?

The classifier that separates classes with the widest possible margin; only the support vectors decide the boundary.

Why the dual?

Data appear only as inner products, so a kernel gives curved boundaries; constraints become a simple box.

Why RBF?

NoGas and Perfume overlap in a curved way; RBF beat linear (84.79%) and polynomial (95.52%).

Why scale?

RBF uses distance; MQ8 varies 3× more than MQ6 and would dominate.

Is it real-time?

1.1 ms per reading against a 2 s sampling interval.

Main limitation?

Sensor drift: 54% on later readings. Needs recalibration or drift compensation.

Where is the pipeline?

The real setting: risers, regulating stations and factory gas lines. Tested on real gas-pipe leak sounds: 88.35% on leak pressure.

Limits of the pipe test?

Compressed air, one rig, and every recording is a leak, so it measures leak strength, not leak or no leak.
